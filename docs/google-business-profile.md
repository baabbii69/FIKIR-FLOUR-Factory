# Google Business Profile — everything to enter

Copy-paste content for **https://business.google.com**. Every fact here comes
from the CMS (`src/generated/content.json`), not from guesswork — so the
profile, the website and the structured data all agree, which is itself a
ranking signal.

For searches like *"flour factory Adama"* or *"biscuit manufacturer Ethiopia"*,
this profile matters **more than the website**. It is what puts the company on
Google Maps and in the panel on the right of the results page.

## 6. Business description

742 of the 750 characters allowed. Paste exactly:

```
Fikir Food Processing is a wheat flour miller and biscuit manufacturer in Adama, Oromia, Ethiopia. Founded in 2011, we produce fortified wheat flour and the Unic range of biscuits, cream wafers and chips, with a team of over 1,000 people.

Our flour is fortified up to Vitamin B12 and licensed under Ethiopia's National Fortified Food scheme, carrying the Ethiopian Standards Mark to CES 309:2024 from the Institute of Ethiopian Standards.

We supply wholesalers, distributors and retailers across Ethiopia through our own delivery fleet. Our range covers Special, 3F, 1K and Corn Flour, alongside 12 Unic biscuit flavours, four wafers and three chip flavours.

Contact us for wholesale orders, distribution partnerships or product enquiries.
```

Why it is written this way: the first sentence carries the terms people
actually search — *wheat flour*, *biscuit manufacturer*, *Adama*, *Ethiopia* —
because Google weights the opening most heavily. The certification paragraph is
the trust signal a wholesale buyer is looking for. Nothing is exaggerated, so
nothing can be contradicted by the website.

## After it goes live

**Get reviews.** Ask distributors and long-standing wholesale customers. Five
genuine reviews will move you further up local results than any change to the
website. Never buy reviews — Google detects and penalises it.

**Post monthly.** New flavours, certifications, hiring, plant milestones.
Dormant profiles slip down.

**Answer questions.** Seed the Q&A yourself with the ones buyers actually ask:

- *Do you supply wholesale quantities?*
- *Do you deliver outside Adama?*
- *Is your flour fortified?*
- *How do I become a distributor?*

**Keep it consistent.** Name, address and phone must match the website exactly
everywhere they appear — the profile, Facebook, AddisBiz, directory listings.
Mismatches actively weaken local ranking.
